Ask on WhatsApp
Which islands do you work on?

All 8 Canary Islands. Our base is in Las Chafiras, Tenerife.

How do I request a visit or a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +34 627 52 55 17. You can also email controlpluscanarias@gmail.com.

Do you issue pest control certificates?

Yes. We issue official certificates for the treatments we carry out. Food, hospitality, retail and industrial businesses subject to health inspection usually need them.

Are you registered in the official biocide register?

Yes. Control Plus Canarias is registered in the Registro Oficial de Establecimientos y Servicios Biocidas de Canarias (ROESBCA).

Which pests do you treat?

Rats, mice, cockroaches, ants, fleas, flies and mosquitoes. Also pigeons and other birds, and woodworm and termites in wood.

Do you provide HACCP plans and training for restaurants?

Yes. We write tailored HACCP plans and run food-handler training for hospitality, shops and food industry.

Can you handle Public Health permits for us?

Yes. We manage registrations, health authorisations and prior notifications with the Dirección General de Salud Pública.
